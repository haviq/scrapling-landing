Capabilities

Everything you need to collect data at scale.

A single dashboard for extraction, lead generation, monitoring and export.

Universal scraper

Extract from any page with simple CSS selectors, or let it detect the main content for you. Text, links, images, tables, attributes.

Local lead finder

Pull business leads from map search results: names, addresses, phone numbers, categories and websites, ready for outreach.

Scheduled monitoring

Run any scrape on an interval. Scrapling detects what changed since the last run and tells you, so you never chase stale data.

Bulk operations

Feed thousands of URLs at once. Parallel workers grind through the queue while you watch live progress and stats.

Instant export

Download results as CSV, Excel or JSON the second a job finishes. Clean columns, no post-processing needed.

Telegram alerts

Get a message the moment a batch completes or a monitored page changes. Stay in the loop without watching a screen.

How it works

From URL to dataset in three steps.

01

Point

Give Scrapling a URL or a list of them. Pick what you want with a selector, or let auto-detect handle it.

02

Collect

Parallel workers fetch and parse everything. Watch progress live. Results are normalized and deduplicated automatically.

03

Use

Export to CSV or Excel, or schedule it to keep running. Get notified when something changes.
